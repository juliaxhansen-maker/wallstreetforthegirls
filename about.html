What this is

One market story a week, explained without the jargon.

Wall Street for the Girls is a weekly finance newsletter that takes the biggest market story of the week and explains it the way you'd explain it to a friend over coffee: no jargon walls, no assumptions you already have a finance degree. Just what happened, why it matters, and what it means for your money.

Every issue starts with the story everyone is talking about and refuses to leave it half-explained. A tariff headline, an earnings beat, a currency intervention, whatever it is, the issue walks through the mechanics underneath it, sorts the signal from the noise, and finishes with the teaching moment most coverage skips. If a term shows up, it gets defined. If a number shows up, it gets worked through. That's the whole promise.

Finance has a habit of talking over the people it affects most. This newsletter exists because none of it is actually too complicated; it's just rarely explained like someone wants you to understand it.
